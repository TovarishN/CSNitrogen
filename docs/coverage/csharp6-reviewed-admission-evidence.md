# CSharp6 admission candidate

CSharp6 has an independent admission guard for the pinned ECMA-334 sixth-edition normalized syntax snapshot. The guard requires an admitted CSharp5 predecessor, the actual 41-module CSharp6 factory, frozen Unicode4 scalar continuity and named reviewer approval of the exact ledger. The selected factory and all admission inputs are listed in the [ledger](csharp6-coverage-gate.json). The guard also checks the 436 source rules and their owner mappings, 1,115 linked witnesses, the Annex A comparison, and all 69 main-text dispositions.

The candidate covers syntax and the source-local restrictions in the [context review](csharp6-normative-context-review.md). Its exclusions include binding and types, modifier compatibility, control flow, task and caller semantics, interpolation conversions, static import lookup and three recorded parser differences. An inherited ambiguity rejects a standalone `await x;` in an async body. The parser accepts a semicolon accessor on an ordinary non-abstract indexer, and it accepts `\U0000005F` at the start of an identifier. The [reference policy](csharp6-reference-policy.json) lists the exact exclusions. The [conformance evidence](csharp6-historical-reading-review.md) records the limits of positional witness inheritance.

CSharp6 stays partial until a named reviewer approves. After inspecting the ledger and its inputs, record approval with `python3 tools/approve-csharp6-review.py --reviewer NAME --ledger-sha256 DIGEST`. The tool checks the supplied digest and all pins before writing the record. The guard requires a new approval if the ledger or a pinned input changes. CSharp7–14, the 7.x minor profiles and custom profiles stay partial.

The candidate passed 12,802 solution tests with zero failures or skips. A separate build with a temporary synthetic approval passed the 21 focused admission and canonical coverage tests, including the public profile and parser entry points. The temporary record was removed. The dependency manifest verified all 141 sources and the owner, witness and main-text disposition generators reproduced their outputs.

The independent code review identified three issues. A malformed ledger now leaves earlier profiles usable and CSharp6 partial. The guard rejects a valid witness from the wrong predecessor rule or alternative position. The approval scope records all three known parser differences. The malformed-ledger build passed all 69 fifth contract tests, and the full suite includes the reproduced witness substitution.
